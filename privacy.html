Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Green Bay Autism Respite Care ("we," "us") collects through greenbayautism.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, call or text us at (920) 301-8093.

Information you give us

When you use the "Send us a message" form, you may give us:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address, if you choose to include it
  • Whether you are a parent or guardian, caseworker, agency, or caregiver applicant
  • Your town and the type of care you're asking about
  • Anything you write in the message box
Please don't send medical records, diagnoses, insurance or Medicaid numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive health or financial details through the website. We'll talk about those by phone.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics, a service from Google, to understand how people use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to collect information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, the type of device and browser you use, how you found our site, and your general location (city level). It also records when someone taps our phone, text, or "Send Message" buttons. This information is reported to us in summary form and does not tell us your name.

You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can stop Google Analytics from collecting your information by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser settings. We do not use Google Analytics advertising features.

How we use your information

  • To answer your message and talk with you about respite care
  • To match your family with a caregiver and coordinate with the service your caseworker has arranged, when you ask us to
  • To consider caregiver applicants
  • To improve our website and understand which areas we're reaching
  • To keep our website and form safe from spam and misuse

We will only contact you about the request you made. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not send marketing emails or texts unless you ask us to.

How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With caregivers, caseworkers, or agencies involved in arranging the care you ask about, and only as needed to arrange that care
  • With service providers who help run our website and messages, such as Google (form delivery, email, and analytics) and GitHub (website hosting)
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety of a child, young adult, or anyone else

How long we keep it

We keep messages from the website only as long as we need them to respond to you and provide services, and for our normal business records. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

Children's privacy

Our services are for children and young adults, but this website and its contact form are meant for parents, guardians, caseworkers, agencies, and adult caregiver applicants. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information through the website, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping your information safe

Our website uses a secure (https) connection, and messages are stored in password-protected accounts. No website or email system is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive health or financial information through the form.

Your choices

  • You can ask what information we have from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by calling or texting (920) 301-8093.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.
  • You can turn off or delete cookies in your browser, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on mentioned above.

Links to other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as Facebook. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
