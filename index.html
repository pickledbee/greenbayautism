How it works

Getting respite care is easy

Three easy steps from your first call to a caregiver your family trusts.

Reach out

Call, text, or send us a message any time, day or night. Tell us a little about your child or young adult and your family's needs.

We find your match

We match your family with a caregiver who fits your loved one's personality, routines, and needs, and coordinate with your caseworker's arranged service.

You get a break

Your caregiver arrives ready to help. You get time to rest, run errands, or just breathe, knowing your loved one is safe and cared for.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is respite care?

Respite care gives parents and family caregivers a break. A trained caregiver spends time with your child or young adult so you can rest, take care of errands, or spend time with the rest of your family.

Does it cost anything?

There is zero cost to families and providers. We work directly with the service your caseworker has arranged.

What ages do you serve?

We provide care for children ages 12 to 17 and young adults ages 18 to 28, along with personal care and daily living skills support.

What areas do you cover?

We serve families from Oshkosh north to Sturgeon Bay. That includes Green Bay, De Pere, Ashwaubenon, Howard, Suamico, Appleton, Kaukauna, Kimberly, Little Chute, Menasha, Neenah, Oshkosh, Shawano, Clintonville, Oconto, Chilton, Waupaca, Marinette, Kewaunee, Manitowoc, Two Rivers and Sturgeon Bay. By county, that's Brown, Outagamie, Winnebago, Calumet, Shawano, Waupaca, Oconto, Marinette, Kewaunee, Manitowoc and Door counties.

When can I reach you?

We're open 24/7. Call or text (920) 301-8093 any time, or send us a message using the form below.

Are you taking new families right now?

Yes! We are currently accepting new clients. Reach out today and we'll start finding the right caregiver for your family.
